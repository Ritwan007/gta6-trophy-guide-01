    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay, no New Game Plus. The checklist appears only after the credits — but everything you do during the story already counts toward it.

    Read this first — the one possibly missable trophy.

    In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony at Jason’s house and accept her meditation. That moment is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy Positive Mental Attitude needs it. Do it, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there are 15 manual save slots plus one autosave, and no mission replay to fall back on.

      Two habits that protect the run.

      Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off the whole run. And the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent and makes the police more aggressive — avoid piling up profile actions you do not need.

      Missables

      Built from the official descriptions and the two full guides that played the game. Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable — and even that one is unconfirmed.

      Everything else: safe in free roam

      Every other trophy is reported as obtainable after the story in free roam. No trophy needs a difficulty setting, a mission medal, or any online play, because the game has none of those.

      Not confirmed yet — do not trust a method with no source

      Five trophies have no confirmed unlock method as of 20 November 2026. This guide says so plainly instead of guessing. Check the sources again before you chase them.

      The 100% checklist — A Long Road

      The gold trophy A Long Road is the hardest one and the last big gate. The checklist has 35 requirements and shows up after the credits under Progress › Stats › 100% Completion. Most items need only part of a set — the ones marked all need everything.

      Counts: PowerPyx 100% Completion Checklist and GTABase 100% Completion Guide, linked on the Sources tab.

      Sources

      Every guide in this atlas names its sources beside it. The two full guides below were written from playing the game and cover all 55 trophies; the rest corroborate the list, the totals, and the 100% checklist. Nothing here copies their words — each guide is written for this atlas.

      What we could not verify

      Rockstar has published no trophy table of its own. The kit names Exophase and PSNProfiles as the list’s origin, but their GTA VI trophy pages could not be opened and verified on 19–20 November 2026, so this atlas does not link them. GamesRadar+, PC Gamer and Press Start printed the same list and checklist on 18 November 2026; their pages are named here without links because their addresses were not verified directly. Where two sources disagree, this atlas follows PowerPyx and GTABase and says so — the known disagreement is the final mission’s chapter, which the two guides place differently.
